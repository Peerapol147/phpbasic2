# Thailand Local Market: Money-Making Coding Opportunities for a Solo Dev + AI Coding Agent (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-22. Method: web search snippets (WebFetch was blocked for most Thai/vendor domains in this session: hdmall.co.th, stripe.com, lineforbusiness.com could not be fetched). Most numbers below therefore come from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Verify prices on the vendor pages before you rely on them.

## 1. Freelance demand on Thai platforms (Fastwork, Fiverr/Upwork from Thailand)

### Takeaway
On Fastwork, LINE chatbot / Messaging API work is a well-stocked category with clear tiered prices. Basic keyword bots with a Rich Menu sell for about 1,500-2,000 THB. Bots with a database or Google Sheet link sell for about 2,500-5,000 THB. AI/OCR/slip-verification bots start around 8,000-15,000 THB. Many sellers compete here, and Fastwork takes a sizeable commission (17% standard).

### Cited Findings
- Fastwork LINE bot listing: a "basic chatbot" package (Messaging API, Rich Menu, Flex Message) costs 1,500 THB with 2-day delivery. The same seller charges 2,500 THB for a package that connects to MySQL or Google Sheet. — [Fastwork gokusenz](https://fastwork.co/user/gokusenz/chatbot-67145390)
- Another seller's "LINE Chatbot (Basic)" costs 2,000 THB over 7 days. It covers keyword Q&A and up to 10 Rich Menu items, with no external system integration. — [Fastwork kasidatep](https://fastwork.co/user/kasidatep/chatbot-68493302)
- A custom LINE bot seller starts at 5,000 THB and includes 12 months of free after-sales support. — [Fastwork sauria](https://fastwork.co/user/sauria/chatbot-39105183)
- A bot that connects AI, ML, OCR and slip verification ("เช็คสลิป") starts at about 12,000 THB. Search summaries put complex backend/AI bots at about 8,000-12,000 THB and enterprise packages at about 15,000 THB. — [Fastwork botline](https://fastwork.co/user/botline/chatbot-15863148)
- Other active sellers in the same category list LINE Chatbot together with LINE Notify, Apps Script, Telegram bots, Flex Message, AI chatbots and Rich Menu, which shows the space is crowded. — [Fastwork meenmin](https://fastwork.co/user/meenmin/chatbot-52925681), [Fastwork djdaith](https://fastwork.co/user/djdaith/chatbot-14764202), [Fastwork punnathat](https://fastwork.co/user/punnathat/chatbot-21412879), [Fastwork chappk](https://fastwork.co/user/chappk/chatbot-32306918)
- Fastwork has more than 400,000 freelancers. Its business has run at a loss in most years, with 2021 the exception. In Feb 2025 it cut the freelancer commission from 17% to 10% for three months (1 Feb-30 Apr 2025) as a promotion. — [The Standard](https://thestandard.co/fastwork-commission-discount/)
- A G2 review cites 17% as the normal commission and says it does not scale down with job size. — [G2 Fastwork](https://www.g2.com/products/fastwork/pricing)
- One article says graphic design is the most-demanded job type on Fastwork, out of more than 30,000 jobs. The article is undated, so treat it as possibly old. — [Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/social/872)

### Inferences
- An AI coding agent makes a 1,500-5,000 THB LINE bot quick to build, but the low tier is a race to the bottom. Better ground is the 8,000-15,000+ THB tier (AI + database + slip verification + LIFF), or recurring monthly care and hosting fees.
- After the 17% commission, a 2,000 THB job nets about 1,660 THB. Fastwork works best for getting first reviews and a portfolio, after which you move repeat clients to direct billing.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable data on Thai-based sellers' prices or competition on Fiverr or Upwork, and no Fastwork stats on demand by category for IT/programming specifically.
- I could not confirm Fastwork's commission after April 2025, or whether it varies by tier.
- I found no Fastwork prices for LIFF booking systems or Google Apps Script automation specifically.

## 2. Thai SME pain points that small software solves (LINE OA / MINI App / LIFF, booking, marketplace tools, e-Tax, POS, AI bots)

### Takeaway
LINE is the default channel for Thai SMEs (around 56M Thai users). LINE itself is pushing MINI Apps hard, including MINI Eats for restaurants (April 2026), with subscription rather than commission (GP) pricing. Queue/booking tools, clinic chatbots, and multi-marketplace order/stock sync (Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop) are established, paid categories. e-Tax Invoice by Email is a government-backed system for small VAT businesses, but I found no confirmed 2026 mandate.

### Cited Findings
- LINE Thailand put the LINE MINI App at the centre of its 2025 roadmap, alongside AI, at the LINE Thailand Developer Conference 2025 (1,000+ attendees). The same writeup cites 56M+ LINE users in Thailand. — [OKKAMI](https://okkami.com/?p=69886)
- LINE MINI Eats, a restaurant ordering and payment system, launched in April 2026 with a monthly subscription instead of a commission on sales. — [Thairath EN](https://en.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/food/2929580)
- MINI Eats does not take GP (commission), and it lets restaurants keep their own customer data. — [Post Today](https://www.posttoday.com/smart-sme/746520)
- MINI Eats has a free trial and a promotional price of 1,390 THB/month. I could not confirm whether that is the standard rate. — [Brand Inside](https://brandinside.asia/line-mini-eats/)
- MINI Eats has rolled out at more than 80 BEARHOUSE branches. — [Marketeer](https://marketeeronline.co/archives/490648)
- LINE's official developer docs carry a case study on queue management with LINE MINI App (matoca/yoboca). Users hold a place in line remotely and get a LINE alert when it is their turn. — [LINE Developers](https://developers.line.biz/en/docs/line-mini-app/technicalcase/bravetechnology/)
- A vendor says restaurants use mini apps for table booking, pre-ordering, queue tickets and stamp-card loyalty. This is vendor marketing. — [Cipher](https://www.cipher.co.th/en/using-the-line-mini-app)
- A LINE-based queue/booking SaaS lists plans at 499 THB/month (Basic), 1,299 THB/month (Pro) and 3,999 THB/month (Enterprise). — [chat.goragod.com](https://www.chat.goragod.com)
- Known pain point: a clinic LINE booking bot often keeps a separate schedule from the clinic's real calendar, which forces double entry. — [HDmall Jib OS](https://hdmall.co.th/jib-os/resources/139-line-bot-booking-for-clinics/)
- Multi-marketplace ERP tools such as BigSeller (20+ platforms, direct APIs to Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop), OneCart and Osco show demand for order and stock sync. Most of these sources are vendor blogs. — [BigSeller](https://www.bigseller.com/blog/articleDetails/manage-shopee-lazada-tiktok-shop.4538.htm?source=app), [OneCart](https://apps.shopify.com/onecart?locale=th), [Osco](https://apps.shopify.com/osco?locale=th)
- e-Tax Invoice by Email (also called "by TimeStamp") was built by the Revenue Department and ETDA so small operators without IT systems can issue e-tax invoices by email with an ETDA timestamp. — [Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/tech/gadget/1131997), [rd.go.th](https://www.rd.go.th/region/04/saraburi/85/5508.html)
- A vendor claims the Revenue Department is pushing e-Tax Invoice to become the standard for VAT-registered businesses, and links it to the Easy E-Receipt stimulus, which needs e-tax invoices or e-receipts. This is a commercial source, not an official rule. — [Grandlinux](https://www.grandlinux.com/blogs/easy-e-receipt-2569.html)
- Wongnai POS is an established F&B POS competitor. — [Wongnai POS](https://www.wongnai.com/pos/)

### Inferences
- The strongest niches for a solo developer are vertical LINE OA, LIFF or MINI App tools for one industry: clinics, salons, gyms, tutoring, small restaurants. Booking/queue plus reminders, membership/points, slip-verified payment, and sync with Google Calendar/Sheets fit well. You can sell them as setup fee + monthly fee (for example 3,000-15,000 THB setup + 300-1,500 THB/month), priced below or around the 499-1,299 THB SaaS anchors.
- Seller tools for Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop compete with funded ERPs (ZORT, BigSeller). A solo developer should focus on narrow gaps (an AI Thai auto-reply/caption generator, price/competitor monitoring, custom reports to Google Sheets) rather than full order sync.
- e-Tax invoice / Easy E-Receipt tools are a possible niche for VAT-registered SMEs, but the regulatory details must be confirmed with rd.go.th and ETDA first.

### Gaps
- I found no Techsauce or Blognone data on SME adoption rates or survey numbers.
- I could not confirm a 2026 mandate or deadline for e-Tax Invoice.
- I did not check marketplace API access requirements (Shopee Open Platform, TikTok Shop Partner) for individual developers.

## 3. Pricing benchmarks of existing Thai SaaS for SMEs

- Botnoi charges per message, from about 0.12 THB per message. This figure comes from HDmall, a competitor. — [HDmall vs Botnoi](https://hdmall.co.th/jib-os/blog/jib-os-vs-botnoi-pay-per-message/)

## 4. Payments for selling software in Thailand

### Takeaway
Opn Payments (Omise) PromptPay costs 1.65% and Thai cards 3.65%, plus 7% VAT on the fee, with no monthly fee. Stripe operates in Thailand with cards and PromptPay. For a solo developer, plain PromptPay QR to a personal account (free) plus manual or slip-check verification is the lowest-cost start. A gateway makes sense once you need automated subscriptions.

### Cited Findings
- Omise/Opn Thailand: PromptPay 1.65%. — [Omise pricing TH](https://www.omise.co/pricing/thailand)
- Omise card example: 3.65% fee + 7% VAT on the fee, so a 1,000 THB charge costs 36.50 + 2.56 THB. No upfront fees, fixed costs or monthly minimums. — [Omise docs](https://docs.omise.co/how-much-does-omise-cost)
- An aggregator lists Omise mobile banking and direct debit at 10 THB per transaction, and withdrawals at 30 THB (≤2M THB) or 150 THB. These figures may be outdated. — [Statrys](https://statrys.com/blog/best-payment-gateways-in-thailand)
- Omise's 2025 merchant discount campaign applied only to accounts live by 31 Dec 2025. — [Omise campaign](https://omise.co/en/campaign/merchant-discount-campaign-2025)
- Stripe is generally available in Thailand for Thai businesses, with cards and PromptPay. — [Stripe newsroom](https://stripe.com/en-at/newsroom/news/thailand), [Stripe PromptPay docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/promptpay)
- Stripe Thailand Connect: PromptPay works with direct charges but not with "separate charges and transfers". — [Stripe support](https://support.stripe.com/questions/stripe-thailand-support-for-marketplaces)
- Stripe has an FAQ on Thailand account requirements and pricing, but I could not read its content. — [Stripe Thailand FAQ](https://support.stripe.com/questions/thailand-faq)

### Inferences
- PromptPay slip-verification bots are already a paid Fastwork add-on (see section 1). Slip verification is both a product to sell and a cheap way to collect your own payments.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve Stripe Thailand fees (stripe.com was blocked). I also could not confirm whether Stripe Thailand accepts individuals/sole proprietors without a company registration.
- I did not research 2C2P or LINE Pay (Rabbit LINE Pay) merchant fees.

## 5. Tax/legal basics for a Thai individual earning online/foreign income (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
Freelance coding income is usually assessable income under Section 40(2) or 40(8). VAT registration is required within 30 days of exceeding 1.8M THB/year. Banks report accounts with ≥3,000 incoming transfers a year, or ≥400 transfers totalling ≥2M THB, to the Revenue Department. Foreign-sourced income remitted into Thailand from 1 Jan 2024 is taxable. The proposed exemption for income remitted in the year earned or the year after had still not appeared in the Royal Gazette as of Sep 2026. PDPA is actively enforced, with more than 21.5M THB in fines so far.

### Cited Findings
- Freelance commissions or piecework fall under Section 40(2). Online selling is Section 40(8), where you can deduct 40-60% as a flat expense rate, depending on the activity, or actual expenses. — [Thairath 2025 guide](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/personal_finance/financial_planning/2915853), [FlowAccount](https://flowaccount.com/blog/online-selling-taxes/)
- VAT: register and charge 7% when revenue exceeds 1.8M THB a year, within 30 days of crossing the threshold. — [FlowAccount](https://flowaccount.com/blog/online-selling-taxes/), [Chubb](https://www.chubb.com/th-th/articles/personal/online-seller-tax-guide.html)
- Half-year PND.94 return is due 1 Jul-30 Sep if Section 40(5)-(8) income exceeds 60,000 THB in Jan-Jun. — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/personal_finance/financial_planning/2915853)
- e-Payment law (Revenue Code Amendment No. 48, 2019): banks report accounts with ≥3,000 deposits or transfers a year per bank, or ≥400 transactions totalling ≥2M THB. The first report was due 31 Mar 2020. Transfer totals are not automatically treated as income. — [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/278602), [KBank](https://www.kasikornbank.com/th/kwealth/Pages/e-payment-tax.aspx) (these are 2019-2020 sources and I found no later change)
- Foreign income: under Instructions Por.161/2566 and 162/2566, foreign-sourced income remitted by a Thai tax resident from 1 Jan 2024 is taxable. Income earned in 2023 or earlier and remitted later is not taxed. — [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/thailand-new-order-closes-tax-loophole-for-offshore-sourced-income), [PwC tax summaries](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/individual)
- The proposed exemption (no tax if income is remitted in the year earned or the following year) was announced by the Revenue Department in May 2025. As of 14 Sep 2026 it was approved in principle but not published in the Royal Gazette, so current rules still apply. — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/economy/628421), [Thailawonline](https://www.thailawonline.com/th/thai-legal-news/still-not-in-the-royal-gazette-the-foreign-income-remittance-exemption-decree/). KTC describes the new rules as if they already apply, which conflicts with this. — [KTC](https://www.ktc.co.th/article/knowledge/financial/tax-income-from-overseas)
- In 2026 the Revenue Department launched a Foreign Tax Credit calculation tool. Claiming a credit requires proof of remittance. — [Luther](https://www.luther-lawfirm.com/en/newsroom/newsletter/detail/newsflash-july-2026-thailands-revenue-department-launches-a-foreign-tax-credit-tool-for-individuals-with-foreign-income)
- Revenue Department guide on tax for income from abroad. — [rd.go.th GuideTaxFromAbroad](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/porphor/GuideTaxFromAbroad_EN.pdf)
- PDPA: in fiscal year 2025 the PDPC issued administrative fines in 5 cases (8 orders), mostly for inadequate security. Since enforcement began there have been 6 cases and 9 orders, totalling more than 21.5M THB. One large private company was fined 7M THB in Aug 2024. — [Prachachat](https://www.prachachat.net/tech/news-1857267), [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/now/content/1538)
- In Aug 2025 the government directed the PDPC to tighten enforcement, aiming for zero data leaks. — [PRD](https://www.prd.go.th/th/content/category/detail/id/39/iid/411160)

### Inferences
- Thai SME clients who pay as companies may withhold 3% tax on service fees. Keep the 50 Tawi certificates (unverified, from general knowledge).
- Fiverr/Upwork income paid out abroad (for example via Payoneer or Wise) and remitted to Thailand is taxable in the year it is remitted under current rules.
- Scraping competitor prices from Shopee or Lazada product pages is mostly non-personal data. Scraping or storing customers' names, phone numbers and LINE IDs triggers PDPA duties: consent, privacy notice, security. Most fines so far are for poor security, so a bot that stores customer data needs basic safeguards.

### Gaps
- I did not verify official PIT brackets or the exact flat-expense rate for Section 40(2) in 2025-2026. From general knowledge, the 0-150k THB bracket is exempt and 40(1)+(2) gets 50% up to 100k THB, but this is unverified.
- I found no PDPC rulings on web scraping specifically.
- I could not confirm the exemption decree's status as of Nov 2026.
