# Solo, AI-assisted coding businesses in 2025-2026: micro-SaaS, digital products, extensions, payments for a Thailand-based seller

Method note: page fetching (WebFetch) was blocked for most hosts in this session (bigideasdb.com, polar.sh, docs.lemonsqueezy.com, developer.paddle.com could not be opened). Every finding below comes from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not from reading each page in full. Treat exact figures as "reported by the cited page". Research date: 2026-11-22.

## 1. Which micro-SaaS / small tool categories have verifiable revenue for solo builders (2025-2026)?

### Takeaway
Payment-verified data (TrustMRR, built on Stripe/Polar/Paddle/RevenueCat API keys) shows a heavily skewed distribution. About 54% of listed indie startups make $0/month, about 12% clear $1K/month, and a few narrow tools reach $40K+ MRR. The clearest verified wins are narrow utilities (form backends, social-post schedulers) and founders with existing audiences. AI social media tools are the most crowded high-earning category.

### Cited Findings
- TrustMRR verifies revenue by having founders connect a read-only payment-provider key (Stripe on most listings, Polar on some). MRR is shown publicly and updated hourly. It proves revenue, not profit — [TrustMRR startup page](https://trustmrr.com/startup/proventools?metric=mrr); [ExitBid TrustMRR review 2026](https://exitbid.io/blog/trustmrr-review-2026); [TrustMRR open feed](https://trustmrr.com/open)
- Scoutr report (2026) covers 8,817 indie startups whose revenue was read from Stripe, RevenueCat, Polar and Paddle via TrustMRR profiles: **54.0% make $0/month and 12.1% clear $1,000/month**. Scoutr also says a first year without revenue is the normal outcome — [Scoutr indie startup revenue report](https://www.scoutr.dev/blog/indie-startup-revenue-report)
- Scoutr's mortality report says 31% of indie startups earned money and then stopped (same 8,817 sample) — [Scoutr startup mortality report](https://www.scoutr.dev/blog/startup-mortality-report)
- Among startups that earn anything, Scoutr reports the highest median monthly revenue in Marketplace ($691) and Social Media ($625). That page uses 9,063 startups, which does not match the 8,817 count — [Scoutr startup ideas](https://www.scoutr.dev/startup-ideas)
- Named verified examples as of Oct 8, 2026 (secondary roundup citing TrustMRR Stripe data): **Postiz $251,282 MRR, POST BRIDGE $48,915 MRR, Web3Forms $41,789 MRR**. AI social media tools are described as the most crowded high-earning category on the leaderboard — [SuperFrameworks best micro-SaaS ideas 2026](https://superframeworks.com/articles/best-micro-saas-ideas-solopreneurs)
- A March 2026 analysis says a small number of top projects take a disproportionate share of verified revenue, so the median is more representative than the top. One aggregator gives a median tracked MRR of $5.1K. That conflicts with the Scoutr data (54% at $0), probably because of a different sample or a filter that excludes $0 listings — [OneMillionGoal: What 986 solo startups look like](https://www.onemilliongoal.com/p/what-986-solo-startups-actually-look); [IdeaWave TrustMRR](https://ideawave.io/startup/trustmrr)
- Marc Lou, self-reported monthly totals on X (not audited):
  - **Feb 2025:** $111,567 total (CodeFast $73.8K, ShipFast $31.5K, Indie Page $2K, DataFast $1.7K, Zenvoice $512, ByeDispute $510, HabitsGarden $334, PoopUp $28).
  - **Apr 2025:** $68,530.
  - **Jul 2025:** $48,921 (CodeFast $20.6K, ShipFast $18.2K, DataFast $6.1K).
  - Most of his small apps make $30-$700/month, so a famous builder with a large audience still sees most tiny tools earn little — [Marc Lou Feb 2025](https://x.com/marclou/status/1895455160769655148); [Apr 2025](https://x.com/marclou/status/1917560065902665964); [Jul 2025](https://x.com/marclou/status/1950900252481130634)
- Marc Lou reportedly made $1.032M in 2025, about 20% below 2024 — [StealWhatWorks](https://stealwhatworks.com/blogs/news/marc-lou-still-making-money); [YesPress profile](https://yespress.io/marc-lou)
- Pieter Levels reportedly says revenue and traffic are declining across indie hackers, including on his own projects, as large AI platforms absorb use cases — [Cipher Projects: Is AI killing indie hackers](https://www.cipherprojects.com/blog/posts/ai-killing-indie-hackers-channel-shift-2026/); [LevelUp/gitconnected](https://levelup.gitconnected.com/one-of-the-most-successful-indie-hackers-says-ai-killed-the-playbook-716f27f995ec)
- Pieter Levels' fly.pieter.com reportedly hit $1M ARR in 17 days (2025), built with Cursor and Three.js. This is an outlier driven by a very large X audience — [findskill.ai](https://findskill.ai/learn-ai-for-entrepreneurs/)
- An analysis of 19 Starter Story interviews found 5 of 19 founders had zero or minimal coding background and built with AI tools. Cursor-built examples ran $12K-$25K MRR. The typical stack was Next.js, Supabase, Vercel, Stripe and Claude Code/Cursor at about $100-400/month in infrastructure. The figures are self-reported in interviews — [Noverload](https://noverload.com/blog/starter-story-founder-patterns-10k-mrr)
- Reported fast $10K MRR cases: Mattia Pomelli's AI design tool in about 6 weeks, and Gil Hildebrand's Subscribr in 100 days. Both are secondary and self-reported — [Brandon McPeak research hub](https://research.brandonmcpeak.com/solo-founder-playbook)
- BigIdeasDB says solo-developer SaaS typically earns $1K-$50K MRR with a median around $1K-$8K. Its startup counts are inconsistent (2,759 / 2,777 / 3,478), and the median likely excludes $0 products — [BigIdeasDB solo developer examples](https://bigideasdb.com/solo-developer-saas-monthly-revenue-examples); [BigIdeasDB micro-SaaS trends 2026](https://bigideasdb.com/micro-saas-trends-2026)

### Inferences
- A realistic base rate for a new solo product is a coin flip on whether it ever earns $1, and roughly a 1-in-8 chance of reaching $1K/month. Among products that do earn, the medians are hundreds of dollars a month, not thousands.
- Verified high earners tend to be narrow "boring" infrastructure or utilities (form endpoints, social cross-posting) or founders who already have an audience. The audience matters more than the AI-built code.
- The portfolio approach (Marc Lou, Levels) works because of one or two hits plus an audience. Most items in a portfolio earn under $500/month.

### Gaps
- I could not open TrustMRR category pages directly to list more solo examples by category with dates.
- I found no verified category breakdown for Chrome extensions or API products in the Scoutr/TrustMRR data.

## 2. Digital products: templates, boilerplates, UI kits, prompt packs, Claude Code skills/MCP, n8n templates

### Takeaway
A few creators earned large sums: Easlo, Thomas Frank, ShipFast, and a $99 Claude skills pack. The typical seller earns close to $0. Generic templates and prompt packs are saturated, and boilerplates have declined as AI agents generate scaffolding. Niche, workflow-specific, B2B-oriented products sold to an existing audience do best.

### Cited Findings
- **Boilerplates (ShipFast).**
  - Early growth: $40K in its first month (Sept 2023) and about $250K in its first 5 months — [startupfounderstories](https://startupfounderstories.com/stories/marc-lou-shipfast-250k-5-months)
  - 2025 decline, from Marc Lou's self-reports: $31.5K (Feb), $22.3K (Apr), $18.2K (Jul) — [Marc Lou on X](https://x.com/marclou/status/1950900252481130634)
  - Current figure: sources conflict. One says about $20K MRR in Dec 2025, while Marc's own bio and other dashboards reportedly show $2K-$4K/month — [StealWhatWorks](https://stealwhatworks.com/blogs/news/marc-lou-still-making-money)
  - His course CodeFast out-earned ShipFast, which suggests teaching people to build sells better than a code template once AI writes the scaffolding — [Marc Lou Feb 2025](https://x.com/marclou/status/1895455160769655148)
- **Notion templates.**
  - Easlo: $239K from template sales in 2022, plus unverified secondhand claims of $500K+/year — [internetbacon](https://internetbacon.com/articles/how-to-create-sell-notion-templates.html); [greyjournal](https://greyjournal.net/hustle/how-to-sell-notion-templates/)
  - Thomas Frank: $1,000,508 in template revenue in 2022, with another claim of $2.1M over about 2 years. These are older data, and both creators had large YouTube or X audiences — [ezycourse](https://ezycourse.com/blog/platforms-to-sell-notion-templates)
  - Typical sellers: realistic earnings run from $0 to about $3K/month, and the median seller with one template and no distribution makes close to nothing. Generic templates are described as saturated — [decidenett 2026 guide](https://decidenett.blogspot.com/2026/10/notion-templates-business-sell-guide.html)
  - SendOwl, a vendor, claims the market is "far from saturated" and that a polished first template earns $200-$800 in its first 3 months — [SendOwl](https://www.sendowl.com/blog/tips-and-advice/how-to-sell-notion-templates); [SendOwl best-selling categories 2026](https://www.sendowl.com/blog/tips-and-advice/best-selling-notion-template-categories)
  - Individual anecdote: $250 total — [The Mindful Planner](https://themindfulplanner.substack.com/p/how-i-made-250-selling-notion-templates)
- **Claude Code skills / MCP servers (2026).**
  - In a dataset of 120+ startups selling to Claude Code, Cursor, MCP or vibe-coding users, **35.2% report any revenue, 5.5% reach $1K/month and 0.8% reach $10K/month** — [BigIdeasDB: make money with Claude Code](https://bigideasdb.com/how-to-make-money-with-claude-code)
  - A $99 "Skills Stack" page reportedly brought in over $20K by Sept 2026. Simple prompt wrappers reportedly don't sell because developers can write them themselves — [Ryan Doser](https://ryandoser.com/sell-claude-skills/); [agent37](https://www.agent37.com/blog/monetize-claude-code-skills)
  - Hosted MCP server anecdote: $15/month with a 7-day trial, about 12% install-to-paid conversion — [DEV: whoffagents](https://dev.to/whoffagents/mcp-server-monetization-how-i-turned-claude-tools-into-15mo-recurring-revenue-4lka)
  - A suggested MCP pricing pattern is a free tier plus a $19-49/month pro tier. Custom MCP builds for businesses reportedly fetch $8K-$50K+ per project, based on agency pricing — [a8gent](https://a8gent.com/blog/sell-mcp-servers-monetize-guide); [eseospace](https://eseospace.com/blog/where-to-sell-or-list-your-mcp-server/)
  - Marketplace cuts: SkillHQ sellers keep 80-85% (paid via Stripe Connect), and Apify keeps a 20% commission. Anthropic's Connectors Directory accepts remote MCP servers — [SkillHQ](https://skillhq.dev/blog/how-to-make-money-selling-claude-code-skills); [agensi](https://www.agensi.io/learn/how-to-monetize-skill-md-skills-developer-guide-2026)
- **n8n templates.**
  - To sell paid templates on n8n's own marketplace you need verified-creator status, which comes after 3 approved free templates. n8n's Cloud affiliate commission is reportedly 30% for 12 months — [thomas.partners](https://thomas.partners/guides/sell-n8n-workflow-templates)
  - The same guide found **no verified figures** on template creator earnings. The only concrete anecdote is about $3,688 over 6 months on Gumroad (self-reported, promotional) — [thomas.partners](https://thomas.partners/guides/sell-n8n-workflow-templates); [emp0](https://articles.emp0.com/unlocking-new-revenue-streams-n8n-templates-gumroad/)
  - The free n8n library already has thousands of templates (2,172 in Sales alone), which pushes down the value of generic workflows — [n8n sales workflows](https://n8n.io/workflows/categories/sales/)
  - Vendor-claimed price points are $29-$299+ — [emp0](https://articles.emp0.com/unlocking-new-revenue-streams-n8n-templates-gumroad/)

### Inferences
- Digital products have the fastest time to first dollar (days to weeks) and near-zero cost. Income depends almost entirely on audience. The headline case studies (Easlo, Thomas Frank, Marc Lou) all had large followings first.
- Generic templates, prompt packs and "SaaS boilerplate #500" products are saturated or commoditised by AI agents that generate them free. Products that encode domain workflow knowledge (vertical n8n flows, industry-specific Claude skills) or sell to businesses hold up better.
- Developer-tools-for-AI-coders is crowded: under 6% reach $1K/month.

### Gaps
- No verified 2025-2026 revenue for Framer/Webflow template sellers. The Framer marketplace has no public figures in my results.
- No revenue data for UI kits or Etsy digital-product sellers specific to 2025-2026.
- No independent data on prompt-pack sales. All I found was marketing copy.

## 3. Browser extensions and small API products

### Takeaway
Chrome extensions can reach $4K-$10K+ MRR solo (self-reported cases, often $7-8/month price points with 2-8% conversion). Large free user bases often convert poorly. Chrome Web Store has had no native payments since 2021, so you need ExtensionPay/Stripe or a merchant of record.

### Cited Findings
- Tab-manager extension reportedly hit $10K MRR at $7/month, with a 30-day trial converting at 8%. Distribution was mainly Chrome Web Store search, not ads. Source is a podcast summary, unverified — [Indie Hacker Podcast via Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/how-a-solo-dev-reached-10k-mrr-with-a-%247-browser-extension/id1896800838?i=1000773803715&l=ru)
- Real-estate extension reportedly at $4,200 MRR after 4 months. Marc Lou's ByeByeAI extension reportedly at $4K MRR. The Poshmark automation tool Closet Tools reportedly at $38K MRR (solo). All come from listicles, and I couldn't find the underlying study — [nichecheck](https://nichecheck.com/blog/chrome-extension-revenue); [nichecheck vs SaaS](https://nichecheck.com/blog/chrome-extension-vs-saas)
- One founder quoted with 500,000 users and $2,400 MRR, showing that installs do not equal revenue — [nichecheck](https://nichecheck.com/blog/chrome-extension-vs-saas)
- Google deprecated Chrome Web Store payments in Sept 2020 and disabled them by Feb 2021. Third-party monetisation is allowed if disclosed in the listing — [Chrome developers](https://developer.chrome.com/webstore/cws-payments-deprecation); [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/tech/google-chrome-browser-extensions-payments-support-deprecated)
- ExtensionPay (open source, Stripe-based) supports one-time and subscription payments. Its backend was upgraded in March 2025. It requires a Stripe account, which is available to Thai individuals (see section 6) — [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com/); [extpay changelog](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/extpay@3.1.2/CHANGELOG.md)
- API products: Apify takes a 20% commission on actors and APIs. Hosted MCP servers act as a new form of small API product ($15-49/month tiers) — [SkillHQ / agent37 sources above](https://skillhq.dev/blog/how-to-make-money-selling-claude-code-skills); [a8gent](https://a8gent.com/blog/sell-mcp-servers-monetize-guide)

### Inferences
- Extensions have built-in distribution through Chrome Web Store search and low build cost, which suits an AI-agent builder. Low price points mean you need thousands of active users per $1K MRR.
- Extensions that automate a workflow for a paying professional niche (real estate, resellers, recruiters) look better than consumer productivity tools.

### Gaps
- No payment-verified dataset of extension revenue for 2025-2026.
- No hard revenue data for small standalone API products such as RapidAPI sellers.

## 4. How "AI makes building cheap" changed competition and distribution in 2026; which channels still work

### Takeaway
The bottleneck has flipped from building to distribution and validation. AI Overviews have cut informational SEO clicks. What still works is comparison, alternatives and use-case SEO, being genuinely present in niche communities (Reddit), periodic launch spikes (Product Hunt, Show HN), marketplace or store search (Chrome Web Store, template marketplaces), and an owned audience. Generic directories are largely worthless.

### Cited Findings
- One developer's time split moved from 70% building / 30% distribution to 40% / 60%. "Build is no longer the constraint; distribution didn't get faster" — [DEV: lmoncany](https://dev.to/lmoncany/i-vibe-coded-my-saas-faster-than-ever-then-hit-the-real-wall-2ap9); [DEV: validation](https://dev.to/lmoncany/why-vibe-coding-is-making-the-customer-problem-worse-not-better-5290)
- Indie Hackers posts: "the bottleneck flipped", "your build-in-public audience is not your market", and products shipped in a weekend that nobody uses — [IH: bottleneck flipped](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/the-bottleneck-flipped-and-most-indie-hackers-we-havent-noticed-d64a3723b7); [IH: vibe coding isn't helping](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/unpopular-opinion-vibe-coding-isn-t-helping-indie-developers-3b868b3a35); [Product Hunt discussion](https://www.producthunt.com/p/murror/you-shipped-it-in-a-weekend-that-s-exactly-why-nobody-s-using-it)
- Market saturation is creating user skepticism toward new AI tools — [create.xyz](https://www.create.xyz/blog/what-it-means-to-be-an-indie-hacker-in-2026)
- SEO and AI answers:
  - Pew found clicks on traditional results fell from 15% to 8% when an AI summary appeared.
  - Ahrefs estimates AI Overviews are associated with about 58% fewer clicks to the #1 organic result.
  - Evergreen explainers are losing the most ground — [Cipher Projects](https://www.cipherprojects.com/blog/posts/ai-killing-indie-hackers-channel-shift-2026/); [truemargin](https://truemargin.ai/blog/indie-hacker-seo-dead-2026)
- What still works:
  - **SEO:** comparison, alternatives and use-case pages convert best.
  - **Reddit:** niche-subreddit presence is high-impact for launches but carries a ban risk.
  - **Product Hunt / Show HN:** best treated as a quarterly spike.
  - **Generic AI directories:** "mostly dead or scammy, conversion negligible".
  - **Recommended mix:** one compounding channel, one spike per quarter, one genuine community, and small paid tests — [Cipher Projects](https://www.cipherprojects.com/blog/posts/ai-killing-indie-hackers-channel-shift-2026/); [buildinpublic.so](https://www.buildinpublic.so/blog/indie-hacker-marketing); [State of Indie Hacking 2026](https://www.stateofindiehacking.com/state-of-indie-hacking-2026/)
- Counter-view: there is growth in new solo-founded companies, and Greg Isenberg argues there is opportunity in AI-era niches — [Cipher Projects](https://www.cipherprojects.com/blog/posts/ai-killing-indie-hackers-channel-shift-2026/); [StealWhatWorks](https://stealwhatworks.com/blogs/news/indie-hackers-going-extinct)

### Inferences
- For a builder using an AI agent, building is close to free, so the edge has to come from distribution: an existing niche community, local language or market knowledge (for example the Thai market), or a channel with built-in search (Chrome Web Store, n8n or Framer marketplaces, the Claude Connectors Directory).
- Programmatic SEO is riskier now. Pages answering "X vs Y" or "tool for [niche job]" questions, which AI Overviews don't fully answer, are the remaining SEO angle.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on Product Hunt launch-to-revenue conversion in 2026.
- The Forg analysis of 700 indie projects was referenced, but its findings didn't come up in search — [Product Hunt Forg](https://www.producthunt.com/p/forg/i-analyzed-700-indie-hacker-projects-here-s-what-everyone-is-actually-building-in-2026)

## 5. Time to first revenue and share of projects that never earn money

### Takeaway
About 54% of payment-connected indie products sit at $0/month (2026, flat versus 2023-2024). Another 31% earned and then stopped. A first year without revenue is normal. Fast $10K MRR stories (6 weeks to 100 days) are self-reported outliers, usually from founders with audiences.

### Cited Findings
- 54.0% at $0 and 12.1% above $1K/month (n=8,817, 2026) — [Scoutr](https://www.scoutr.dev/blog/indie-startup-revenue-report)
- The 54% zero-revenue rate is reportedly unchanged from 2023 and 2024, per a ScrapingFish analysis of Stripe-verified IH products — [solooperatorstack](https://solooperatorstack.com/blog/indie-hacker-revenue-distribution-tam-clarity/)
- 31% earned money and then stopped — [Scoutr mortality](https://www.scoutr.dev/blog/startup-mortality-report)
- Older data (about 2019): 45.6% of IH projects never earned $1 — [Medium IH dataset](https://medium.com/@neotokio/indiehackers-statistics-dataset-exploration-case-study-7a0089fc4d59)
- AI-dev-tool niche: only 35.2% report any revenue — [BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/how-to-make-money-with-claude-code)
- Anecdote: 47 side projects earned $127 in total — [Medium](https://medium.com/lets-code-future/i-built-47-side-projects-none-made-money-heres-what-i-learned-f462dea726d8)
- Notion template first-3-month expectation is $200-$800 (vendor claim) — [SendOwl](https://www.sendowl.com/blog/tips-and-advice/how-to-sell-notion-templates)

### Inferences
- These samples are biased toward products that launched publicly and connected a payment processor. The true share of never-earning projects, including abandoned ones, is likely higher than 54%.
- Typical time to first dollar is probably days to weeks for digital products with an audience, and months (often never) for SaaS without a distribution plan.

### Gaps
- I found no rigorous median time-to-first-dollar statistic.

## 6. Payment options for a seller based in Thailand

### Takeaway
A Thai individual can open a **Stripe Thailand** account directly as an Individual or Sole Proprietor. It accepts 135+ currencies, pays out in THB only, and settles in 7 business days. **Polar** (MoR) lists Thailand as a payout country. **Gumroad** (MoR since Jan 2025) pays via PayPal or bank. **Lemon Squeezy is shutting down** (new sign-ups closed Nov 16, 2026; payments stop Mar 31, 2027), so it should not be used. Paddle, Creem and Dodo claim broad coverage, but I could not confirm Thailand from their primary docs.

### Cited Findings
- **Stripe Thailand.**
  - Business types include Sole Proprietorship (กิจการเจ้าของคนเดียว) and Individual (บุคคลธรรมดา), using your legal name from your Personal ID. You need a THB-denominated Thai bank account — [Stripe support: info required in Thailand](https://support.stripe.com/questions/what-information-is-required-to-open-a-stripe-account-in-thailand)
  - Eligible: businesses including sole proprietors located in Thailand. Under-20s need a parent or guardian as representative — [Stripe Services Agreement Thailand](https://stripe.com/th/legal/deutsche-bank)
  - Accepts payments in 135+ currencies, but payouts are THB only. Payout schedule is 7 business days — [Stripe support: payout schedule and currency Thailand](https://support.stripe.com/questions/payout-schedule-and-currency-for-stripe-accounts-in-thailand); [Stripe supported payment methods Thailand](https://support.stripe.com/questions/supported-payment-methods-currencies-and-businesses-for-stripe-accounts-in-thailand)
  - Supports cards and PromptPay, with full Billing for subscriptions — [Stripe launches in Thailand](https://stripe.com/cn/newsroom/news/thailand)
  - Some licensed industries can't use Connect Custom/Express in Thailand — [Stripe: Connect restrictions Thailand](https://support.stripe.com/questions/are-there-any-businesses-that-stripe-cant-support-on-connect-in-thailand)
  - Stripe is not a merchant of record, so you handle EU VAT and other foreign sales tax yourself (inference based on Stripe's role as processor).
- **Polar (MoR).**
  - Pays out via Stripe Connect Express to a published country list that includes **Thailand** (aggregator check dated 2026-10-30, 118 payout countries) — [Polar docs supported countries](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries); [getpaidatlas Polar](https://getpaidatlas.com/provider/polar)
  - Polar says that if your country isn't listed at onboarding, it can't pay out there — [Polar on X](https://x.com/polar_sh/status/1915379610809782428)
  - Pricing reported as 5% + 50¢ (2026) — [Dodo Polar review](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/polar-sh-review)
  - Do not confuse it with Polar Fi (polarfi.com), an unrelated service.
- **Gumroad.**
  - Merchant of record since Jan 2025. Fee is 10% + $0.50 on direct sales and 30% on marketplace (Discover) sales.
  - Pays by bank where supported, otherwise by PayPal in USD (1-3 days). Bank payouts take 2-7 business days, and you must prove residence.
  - The PayPal payout fee is reportedly 2% — [Gumroad help: getting paid](https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid); [Dodo: Gumroad fees 2026](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/gumroad-fees-explained); [flaviocopes](https://flaviocopes.com/payment-providers/gumroad/)
  - PayPal Thailand charges 50 THB per withdrawal to a Thai bank. A personal account requires Thai residence and age 20+ — [PayPal TH fees](https://www.paypal.com/th/webapps/mpp/paypal-fees)
  - I did not confirm whether Gumroad supports direct bank payouts to Thailand.
- **Lemon Squeezy.**
  - Winding down. New store sign-ups closed Nov 16, 2026 and payment processing stops Mar 31, 2027. Stores are migrating to Stripe Managed Payments (3.5% + Stripe fees, with a 1.5% promo for migrators through Dec 1, 2027) — [Lemon Squeezy wind-down FAQ](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/migration/faq); [TechStartups Nov 16 2026](https://techstartups.com/2026/11/16/stripe-shuts-down-lemon-squeezy-just-two-years-after-acquisition-giving-businesses-until-march-2027-to-migrate/); [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/lemon-squeezy-is-winding-down-and-will-stop-taking-payments-in-march-2027/)
  - Stripe Managed Payments reportedly accepts businesses in only about 37 locations. Alternatives suggested for Thailand-based software sellers: Paddle, Payhip, Digistore24, Dodo Payments, Creem, Freemius, Fungies — [merchantofrecordfinder](https://www.merchantofrecordfinder.com/blog/lemon-squeezy-shutting-down); [notevibes](https://notevibes.com/blog/lemon-squeezy-shutdown-where-to-move)
  - I could not confirm whether Thailand is among the Stripe Managed Payments locations.
  - Lemon Squeezy's payout list did include Thailand (Oct 2026), but that is moot for new sellers — [flowclique](https://www.flowclique.com/2026/10/lemon-squeezy-supported-countries.html)
- **Paddle.** MoR for digital goods and services, covering sellers in "200+ countries and territories" per its partner docs. ### Inferences
- Simplest compliant setup for selling globally: **Polar or Gumroad** as merchant of record, which handles VAT/GST. Use Gumroad for digital downloads because of its marketplace and simplicity, and accept the 10% fee. Use Polar for SaaS or subscriptions at a lower fee.
- For selling inside Thailand (PromptPay, THB), use **Stripe Thailand** directly as an individual. It also works with ExtensionPay for extensions. Keep in mind the 7-day payout delay and that foreign tax compliance is on you.
- Avoid building on Lemon Squeezy. Check any MoR's current Thailand onboarding before committing, because providers' coverage is changing (see the Lemon Squeezy shutdown).
- Thai personal income tax on foreign-sourced income is outside this scope, but the user should check it.
