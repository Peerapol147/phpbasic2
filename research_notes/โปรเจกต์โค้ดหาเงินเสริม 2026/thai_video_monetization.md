## 1. YouTube Partner Program (YPP) requirements, Thailand availability, and Thai RPM/CPM

### Takeaway
Today's YPP thresholds are 500 subscribers for fan funding and Shopping, or 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours or 10M Shorts views for ad revenue. **On 1 Feb 2027 the ad-revenue entry bar doubles to 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views.** For a channel starting now, that is the most important timing fact. Thai RPM is low: roughly US$0.35-0.55 per 1,000 views on average. Shorts pay only cents per 1,000 views. I found no Thai-specific split between the knowledge and football niches.

### Cited Findings
- Current tiers: 500 subscribers unlocks fan funding. 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours (12 months) or 10M Shorts views (90 days) unlocks ad revenue. The 500-subscriber tier only exists in countries where the expanded program has launched. — [vidIQ YPP guide 2026](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/monetize-youtube-guide/); [AIR Media-Tech YPP 2026](https://air.io/en/monetization/youtube-partner-program-requirements-2026-the-complete-guide); official page: [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?rd=1) (could not fetch)
- Announced August 2026, effective **1 Feb 2027**: new applicants will need 1,000 subscribers plus **8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days or 20M qualified Shorts views in 90 days**. The current rules apply to new applicants until that date. — [YouTube Official Blog: YPP updates 2027](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/?hl=en); [TechTimes 2026-08-11](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323954/20260811/youtube-partner-program-doubles-entry-bar-adds-monthly-shorts-earnings-gate.htm); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-updates-partner-program-terms-shorts-payout-rules/585331/); [NewscastStudio](https://www.newscaststudio.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-monetization-changes-2027/)
- The 2027 terms also add an ongoing Shorts gate. Some sources say every YPP channel will need 10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days to earn Shorts ad revenue. Others describe it as a payout gate rather than a membership rule, so the exact scope is unclear. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-updates-partner-program-terms-shorts-payout-rules/585331/); [Umbrella Creators](https://umbrellacreators.com/blog/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027)
- 2027 "active channel" rule: at least 1,000 qualified watch hours per year, or 1M Shorts views per 90 days, or a minimum number of uploads. Inactive channels get 90 days to recover. Existing partners must accept the new terms by 31 Jan 2027. — [SocialDay](https://socialday.live/features/youtube-raises-partner-program-entry-bar-to-8-000-watch-hours-from-february-2027); [Umbrella Creators](https://umbrellacreators.com/blog/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027)
- Fan funding and Shopping reportedly stay at 500 subscribers plus 3,000 watch hours or 3M Shorts views after 2027. — [Umbrella Creators](https://umbrellacreators.com/blog/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027) (secondary source)
- Dynamoi, a third-party sample of linked channels, puts Thai creator RPM at about **US$0.37 per 1,000 views** for 2 Nov 2025 to 31 Oct 2026. The all-market average is $1.12, and Thailand ranks 53rd of 87 markets. Thai RPM swings by season: about $1.76 in Dec 2025 and about $0.21 in Apr 2026. Average Thai view length was 3.5 minutes, against 11.1 minutes across all markets. Another Dynamoi page gives $0.55 for 2026, which conflicts with the $0.37. — [Dynamoi Creator RPM: Thailand](https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/th); [Dynamoi Thailand RPM article](https://dynamoi.com/learn/youtube-music-promotion/youtube-rpm-thailand)
- Other estimates for Thailand vary widely: CPM from $0.27 up to $6.08 depending on the aggregator. CreatiCalc gives $1-3 CPM and $0.50-1.50 RPM. vidIQ puts Thai audience RPM at about $0.525. bannerTag says its country values are not measured data. — [CreatiCalc by country](https://creaticalc.com/blog/youtube-cpm-rates-by-country); [vidIQ calculator](https://vidiq.com/youtube-money-calculator/); [bannerTag](https://www.bannertag.com/youtube-video-cpm-rates-2026/); [SKL](https://www.skl.com.np/2021/07/youtube-cpm-and-cpc-rates-by-country.html)
- Sports niche, global and mostly US audiences: Qiit gives sports RPM of about $1.5-4 and describes it as a "large audience but low purchase intent" niche. vidIQ estimates US sports RPM at about $4 and CPM at about $12. — [Qiit](https://qiit.com/en/youtube-cpm); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-money-calculator/)
- Shorts RPM globally: typically $0.03-0.08 per 1,000 views, with an average around $0.08 and top channels above $0.30. Shorts revenue comes from a shared pool calculated per country, and each music track cuts the creator's share (one track means 50%). — [TubeAnalytics TH](https://tubeanalytics.net/th/blog/youtube-shorts-monetization-2026); [VoxBooster TH](https://voxbooster.com/th/blog/youtube-shorts-rpm-monetization-statistics-2026/)

### Inferences
- Rough Thai revenue: at about $0.4 RPM, 1M long-form views earn about US$400 (about 13-14k THB). At about $0.02-0.05 RPM, which is plausible given the low global Shorts rate times Thailand's low ad prices, 1M Shorts views earn roughly US$20-50. This is an estimate: I found no Thai Shorts RPM.
- Ad revenue from Shorts alone is not a viable main income in Thailand. Use Shorts to grow and to drive affiliate sales, and earn ad revenue from long-form 16:9 videos.
- **Urgency:** a channel that reaches 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours, or 10M Shorts views, **before 1 Feb 2027** gets in under the old bar. After that date the bar doubles. The new 10M-per-90-day Shorts gate could cut off small Shorts-focused channels even after they join.
- The knowledge/explainer niche probably earns a higher RPM than football, because sports audiences have low purchase intent. Thai football long-form will likely sit at or below Thailand's average RPM.

### Gaps
- No Thai-specific Shorts RPM, and no measured Thai RPM split between the knowledge and football niches. No Thai creator income screenshots from reliable sources.
- I could not confirm from YouTube directly that the 500-subscriber tier is live in Thailand, though Thailand is generally listed as YPP-available.

## 2. YouTube "inauthentic content" policy (2025/2026), AI disclosure, and TikTok AI labelling

### Takeaway
On 15 July 2025 YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" policy to "inauthentic content". The policy covers mass-produced, template-like content that can be copied at scale with little variation. A July 2026 clarification added generic or template content, distressing content, and AI personas discussing topics. Enforcement hits the whole channel. AI voice and AI tools are allowed if the videos add original insight and vary meaningfully. A templated pipeline is exactly the high-risk case.

### Cited Findings
- The 15 July 2025 change was presented as a clarification and rename: "repetitious" became "inauthentic", explicitly covering repetitive or mass-produced content. Such content was already barred from monetization. The help page describes it as content that looks made from a template with little or no variation, or can easily be copied at scale. — [TechCrunch 2025-07-09](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/09/youtube-prepares-crackdown-on-mass-produced-and-repetitive-videos-as-concern-over-ai-slop-grows); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/); [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Enforcement is per channel: if some videos break the rule, the whole channel can lose monetization. AI content can still be monetized if it adds original insight or perspective. — Search summary of [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/09/youtube-prepares-crackdown-on-mass-produced-and-repetitive-videos-as-concern-over-ai-slop-grows) / [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/youtube-targets-mass-produced-content-in-monetization-update/550337/)
- July 2026 update: guidance now groups inauthentic content into (1) generic, repetitive, or template-based content; (2) off-putting or distressing content; (3) **content where AI personas are used to discuss topics**. YouTube says this is not a new policy. — [TechCrunch 2026-07-20](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/); [Tubefilter 2026-07-13](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026-07-13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/)
- Thai media covered the 2025 rule widely. Sanook says using AI as a tool while adding your own ideas and storytelling stays monetizable, but pumping out repetitive videos or running a content farm gets restricted. Marketeer headlined it as paying only for human-voiced videos, which is a misleading simplification. — [Sanook](https://www.sanook.com/hitech/1627598/); [Marketeer Online](https://marketeeronline.co/archives/424594); [Appdisqus](https://www.appdisqus.com/youtube-new-policy-july-2025-ai-content-demonetized/); [iT24Hrs](https://it24hrs.com/2025/youtube-new-rule-crack-down-ai-generated-repetitive-content/)
- In early 2026 YouTube began a large proactive removal of "AI slop" channels and videos, which Modify reports had about 4.7B views. YouTube's CEO named reducing AI slop a 2026 priority. — [Modify.in.th](https://www.modify.in.th/43160)
- AI disclosure, May 2026 update: there is now one standard label for photorealistic or meaningfully AI-altered content. It sits below the player on long-form videos and as an overlay on Shorts. Unrealistic, animated, or slightly altered content is disclosed in the description only. YouTube auto-labels content made with its own AI tools, content carrying C2PA metadata, or content its systems detect as AI. — [YouTube Blog: Improving AI labels](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/improving-ai-labels-viewers-creators/?pubDate=20260527); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/27/youtube-updating-ai-content-labels/)
- Disclosing AI use does not limit reach or monetization. Creators who repeatedly fail to disclose risk forced labels, video removal, or suspension from YPP. — [YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en-GB)
- TikTok: creators turn on an "AI-generated" disclosure toggle, and TikTok also auto-labels content whose metadata (C2PA) shows it is AI-generated. This comes from vendor guides; check TikTok's Transparency Center. — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/ai-disclosure-rules/)

### Inferences
- **What likely gets demonetized:** many near-identical videos from one template, such as the same intro, the same stock b-roll, swapped facts, and one TTS voice reading text that adds no analysis. Football "news" that just reads scores aloud fits this pattern. A synthetic AI host persona is also now explicitly named.
- **What likely passes:** scripts with real research and an editorial viewpoint, custom motion graphics per topic, varied formats, a recurring human-curated angle (for example, an opinion take or tactical analysis), and sensible upload volume. Disclose AI where the content looks realistic. TTS narration over stock footage and graphics usually does not need the prominent label, because it is neither photorealistic nor deceptive. This is my inference; check the help page.
- Because enforcement is per channel, the creator should keep the knowledge and football niches on separate channels so a strike on one does not hit the other.
- Reach is the risk: the YouTube Partner Program review is manual, and pipeline channels are where reviewers look most closely.

### Gaps
- I found no named Thai channel documented as demonetized under the 2025/2026 policy.
- I could not read the full TikTok Community Guidelines for AIGC (AI-generated content). Whether TikTok Thailand demotes unlabelled AI content is unverified.

## 5. Football content: copyright risk, safe formats, monetization limits, seasonality

### Takeaway
League footage, broadcast clips, and logos are enforced aggressively. The Premier League has a long record of taking down unlicensed clips and even struck the 2023 Sidemen charity stream. A no-footage pipeline (stats, animated tactics boards, original graphics, TTS commentary) avoids Content ID matches, but it becomes the "templated / repetitive" content the inauthentic policy targets if every video looks the same. I found no Thai-specific enforcement sources.

### Cited Findings
- The Premier League has fought unlicensed highlights since 2006-07: warning letters to clip sites and a 2007 lawsuit against YouTube. The court limited its US statutory damages in 2009-2010. — [World Soccer Talk 2006](https://worldsoccertalk.com/news/premier-league-issues-warning-to-footage-site-20061021-CMS-419.html); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/internet/web/youtube-sued-over-premiership-football-clips-132898); [World Soccer Talk 2010](https://worldsoccertalk.com/news/court-throws-out-premier-leagues-case-against-youtube-20100626-CMS-21386.html) (**old data**)
- In September 2023 the Premier League copyright-struck the Sidemen Charity Match livestream, even though reports said it contained no PL IP. It was later reinstated. — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/news-why-sidemen-charity-match-2023-video-deleted-youtube-reason-behind-dmca-takedown-explored); [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/sidemen-charity-match-pulled-from-youtube-by-the-premier-league)
- In the EU, football matches as such are not protected by copyright, but broadcast footage and highlight clips are a separate question. — [Kluwer Copyright Blog](https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/copyright-blog/premier-league-claims-copyright-on-football-matches-shown-in-copyright-debate/)
- Thai rights for World Cup 2026: Jasmine International (JAS), streaming on MONOMAX. True held the 2022 rights. The tournament ran 11 June to 19 July 2026. — [Khaosod English](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/news/2026/06/09/jas-secures-thailands-world-cup-2026-broadcast-rights/); [Thairath EN](https://en.thairath.co.th/sport/worldcup/2932262)
- Sports RPM is held back by low purchase intent; most sports money goes to live rights. — [Qiit](https://qiit.com/en/youtube-cpm)

### Inferences
- **Safe or lower-risk:** original analysis scripts, stats graphics built from public data (check data licensing terms; some providers such as Opta-type feeds are licensed), animated tactics boards with generic kits, transfer-rumour roundups with sources cited, predictions, history and "legend" explainers, and player-ratings formats. Use generic team colours instead of crests, and use stock or licensed player photos only (or none). Player likenesses and AI-generated photorealistic players raise likeness and disclosure issues.
- **Unsafe:** any broadcast clips, even short ones; screen-recordings; league logos, crests, and broadcaster logos; official music; and "highlights" thumbnails using match frames. These are explicitly prohibited by the creator's own constraints.
- Ad-friendliness: betting mentions are a problem. Gambling content is heavily restricted in Thailand and on YouTube ads, so avoid betting tips and odds.
- Seasonality: European leagues run August to May, with dips in June and July outside tournament years. Thai League 1 runs roughly August to May. Traffic peaks on match days and transfer deadlines (late August and late January). Thai RPM also peaks in Q4 (Dec 2025 about $1.76 RPM per [Dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/th)) and falls in Q1 and Q2. The next big tournament spike is not until World Cup 2030 or EURO 2028.

### Gaps
- No Thai sources found on Thai League or broadcaster (True, JAS/MONO, Thai League Co.) Content ID enforcement, and nothing on how Thai copyright fair-use defences (การใช้โดยชอบธรรม) have fared for commentary.
- No Thai football traffic seasonality data found; the seasonality above is my inference from league calendars.

## 7. Additional income streams (pipeline as a service, sponsorships, Facebook)

### Takeaway
Facebook Content Monetization (invite or apply via Professional Dashboard) has replaced the Reels Play bonus, but Thailand's eligibility is unconfirmed. Meta's Shopee affiliate tie-in is live in Thailand. Selling the pipeline as a service to Thai SMEs is plausible; per-clip price benchmarks are thin.

### Cited Findings
- Facebook ended the Reels Play Bonus globally on 31 Aug 2025 (one guide says 2023, which conflicts). Facebook Content Monetization pays for Reels, longer videos, Stories, and photo and text posts. It is invite-based, and creators can express interest in the Professional Dashboard. — [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40051146); [Facebook Creators (TH)](https://creators.facebook.com/earn-money/expand-your-opportunities-to-earn/?locale=th_TH)
- Meta paid creators nearly $3B in 2025, 60% of it from Reels. In March 2026 Meta launched Creator Fast Track, which pays TikTok and YouTube creators up to $3,000 a month to post Reels; country availability is unconfirmed for Thailand. — [Meta Newsroom 2026-03](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/cre); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/facebook-creator-fast-track)
- One third-party guide lists Thailand as having "partial" access to Facebook Content Monetization. This is unverified. — [cavafy guide](https://cavafy.onassis.org/?p=138089)
- SME video service benchmarks: AI tools cost $10-30 a month for a solo creator producing 25-50 short videos. Freelance editors cost $500-3,000 a month and agencies $2,000-10,000 a month (not Thailand-specific). A Bangkok agency on Clutch charges $50-99 an hour. One Thai media AI case study cut production from 3-4 weeks to under 1 week per episode. — [GoFaceless TH](https://www.gofaceless.ai/th/blog/economics-of-ai-video-production-costs); [Clutch TH](https://clutch.co/th/agencies/video-production/commercial); [AnyMind](https://anymindgroup.com/?p=58610)

### Inferences
- **Highest expected value for this creator:** a B2B service selling Thai-language explainer and product videos (Thai TTS, motion graphics) to SMEs and TikTok Shop sellers on a monthly retainer.
